How to Play

Simple matches, sweet companionship

Five easy steps take you from your first swap to a puzzle world full of pets.

  • 1

    Match Candy Tiles

    Swap and match three or more candies to clear the board.

  • 2

    Unlock Pet Companions

    Collect adorable pets as you progress through levels.

  • 3

    Use Pet Abilities

    Each companion offers a unique boost during gameplay.

  • 4

    Clear Level Goals

    Complete objectives to earn rewards and advance.

  • 5

    Progress Through Puzzle Worlds

    New levels bring fresh challenges and pets to discover.
